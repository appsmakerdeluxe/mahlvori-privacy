DACH product catalog

Mahlvori · Updated 18 October 2026

This is an offline-ready derived selection from Open Food Facts. It contains product names, GTINs and community-reported retailer tags; no product photos, prices, branch stock or private user data.

Download DACH catalog (ZIP)

Scope and source

66,632 products with valid GTIN check digits, filtered to Germany, Austria and Switzerland and records carrying retailer tags. The selection includes tags such as EDEKA, REWE, ALDI, Lidl, Kaufland, PENNY, Netto, BILLA, Hofer, SPAR, Migros, Coop and Denner. It is not a complete retailer or branch assortment.

Source: Open Food Facts, Parquet data pinned to commit 69acdceb31a30d23310c72120442d6d7d09e2d73. The download contains the actual SQLite database distributed in the app and its build report.

Retailer data limitations

Retailer tags are community maintained and may be absent, incorrect or outdated. They do not prove that a product is currently listed or available at a particular branch. Mahlvori does not show live offers or stock. Product photographs are not part of this data set and are not copied from the OFF catalog.

The catalog is separate from personal recipes, allergy settings, pantry, prices and shopping lists. The app does not send barcode scans or searches to Open Food Facts.
