Planned Pro Privacy Notice

18 October 2026 · app not yet released

Pro is in preparation. This planned notice will become final only after the app, exports and actual data handling have been decided.

Who is responsible?

AppsMakerDeluxe · appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

Planned data processing

Mahlvori Pro is planned as a standalone offline Android app. On device it will process meal plans, recipes, servings, pantry items, prices and personal preferences. Multi-week plans, reusable templates, cooking schedules and shopping lists are planned. No account, advertising, app analytics, tracking, cloud sync or remote app server is planned.

The camera would start only after a deliberate action for the optional barcode scanner. Android system pickers would be used for user-initiated file import and export. Backup contents, potentially sensitive free-form text and any external sharing will be documented after they are implemented.

Separate app purchase

A future one-time purchase would be handled through Google Play. AppsMakerDeluxe does not receive card or payment credentials through the app. The final purchase flow will be documented before sale.

Storage and deletion

Local storage and user-started file transfer are planned. The Pro backup format, encryption and deletion controls have not yet been implemented or finalized; they will be documented before sale. Do not treat this page as a completed release policy.
