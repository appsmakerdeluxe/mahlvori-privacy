Privacy Policy

Mahlvori · 18 October 2026 · planned, app not yet released

This page describes the planned free Mahlvori app. Before release, the policy will be reconciled with the actual app and included libraries.

Who is responsible?

AppsMakerDeluxe
Contact: appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

Data handled by the app

The planned app stores its meal-planning information only on your Android device: country, serving size, optional weekly budget, preferred shopping locations, meal plans, your recipe notes, food preferences, exclusions and allergens, kitchen equipment, pantry, shopping list and prices you save yourself.

There is no user account, telemetry or automatic sync. These personal app records are not sent to AppsMakerDeluxe or to advertising and analytics providers.

Camera and barcodes

The camera is used only if you open the barcode scanner and grant Android permission. The bundled barcode recognition analyzes the image on-device; it is not uploaded or saved for another purpose. Recognized codes are searched locally in the product catalog. Manual item entry remains available.

Location, stores and prices

The planned app does not need device location. Shopping-store labels organize your list; the app does not access retailer accounts or stock records. Offline reference prices and your own entries are estimates or personal records, not live offers. Unknown values remain unknown; prices are not sent to stores.

Backups, sharing and deletion

Automatic cloud backups are not planned. If you export or import a backup, Android's file picker lets you choose the destination or file. A shopping list is handed to another app only if you deliberately use Android's share action. The receiving app processes it under its own terms.

You can erase local Mahlvori records through the app or Android app settings. Removing the app also removes its local app data. Exported files must be removed separately by you.

Open Food Facts and product catalog

The prepared app includes an offline DACH selection of product names, GTINs and community-reported retailer tags for Germany, Austria and Switzerland. It contains no product photographs, prices or branch stock and is stored separately from personal data. The distributed database, its source, ODbL/DbCL notices and limitations are available in the data and license section. Barcode scans and product searches are not sent to Open Food Facts.

Security and third-party code

The app is designed for local processing. Before release, the included software libraries and their actual data handling will be reviewed and this page will be updated accordingly. No remote app server is planned.

Updates and privacy contact

Development can change this planned data handling. Updated text will carry a new date. Questions: appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com.
